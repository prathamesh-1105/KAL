💡 Pro Tips:

  • Always use target="_blank" for Google Form links
  • Keep show descriptions short (2-3 lines max)
  • Use good quality images (400x200 pixels)
  • Test your Google Form before linking
  • Keep consistent date format (e.g., "July 20, 2025")
